# Data

* `synthetic_xtf/`: **main synthetic campaign**: 56 XTF survey lines in 7 areas off the Indian coast, with navigation,
  depth, attitude, CTD and full ground truth (labels in lat/lon, pixels and YOLO format). See `synthetic_xtf/README.md`.
* `v1/`: first 3 simple synthetic surveys (made by `ai/tools/generate_xtf_v1.py`), kept for reference.

All files here are simulated, not real recordings.
